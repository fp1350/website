Day Trading AI Syllabus

30 questions to ask AI, organized by topic. Each entry: the question, why it matters, and what to walk away knowing.

Module 1 · Foundations refresher
01

"Quiz me on Level 2 order book reading — show me 5 example scenarios and have me predict the next move."

Why: Reading the tape separates guessers from traders. Active practice beats passive reading.

Outcome: Recognize bid/ask imbalance, spoofing, hidden orders, and absorption in real time.

02

"Explain the difference between market makers, ECNs, and dark pools — and how each affects my fills."

Why: Understanding who's on the other side of your trade changes how you place orders.

Outcome: Smarter order routing decisions, fewer surprise slippages.

03

"What are the exact rules around the PDT (Pattern Day Trader) designation, and what are real strategies to work around it?"

Why: The $25K rule wrecks small accounts that don't plan around it.

Outcome: Know how to structure trades, use cash accounts, or multiple brokers legally.

04

"Walk me through the full anatomy of a trade — from scan to entry to exit — for a momentum setup."

Why: A clear mental model for the whole trade lifecycle reduces hesitation.

Outcome: A repeatable checklist she can apply every morning.

05

"What's the difference between float, share structure, and short interest — and how do they affect intraday price action?"

Why: Low-float runners trade nothing like large caps. Wrong assumption, wrong stop.

Outcome: Match strategy to ticker type.

Module 2 · Technical analysis
06

"Quiz me on candlestick patterns — describe one and have me name it, then have me describe one for you to name."

Why: Two-way practice locks in pattern recognition far faster than flashcards.

Outcome: Instant recognition of hammers, engulfing, dojis, harami, and their context.

07

"Explain VWAP — how it's calculated, who uses it, and three different ways to trade off it."

Why: VWAP is the most important intraday level institutions watch. Anchor for entries and exits.

Outcome: VWAP bounces, VWAP rejections, VWAP reclaim setups.

08

"What are the most reliable chart patterns for day trading, and what's the failure rate of each?"

Why: Knowing the failure rate matters as much as the pattern itself.

Outcome: Bull flags, ABCDs, opening range breakouts — with realistic expectations.

09

"How do I read volume properly? What does volume tell me that price doesn't?"

Why: Price without volume context is half the picture.

Outcome: Spot exhaustion, climactic volume, dry-ups, and accumulation.

10

"Explain support and resistance like I've never heard of them — then teach me what actually creates them."

Why: Most traders draw lines on charts without understanding the order flow behind them.

Outcome: Understanding stop clusters, liquidity pools, and why levels actually hold.

11

"What indicators are actually useful for day trading and which are noise? Explain the math behind RSI and MACD."

Why: Most new traders stack indicators. Knowing what each one actually measures kills the clutter.

Outcome: A minimalist chart with intentional tools.

Module 3 · Strategy & setups
12

"Compare gap-and-go, opening range breakout, and VWAP reclaim — entry, stop, target, and conditions for each."

Why: Different markets reward different strategies. Picking the right one matters.

Outcome: A toolkit of 3 setups with crystal-clear rules.

13

"Help me build a pre-market routine — what should I check between 7am and 9:30am?"

Why: Most edge is built before the bell, not during the chaos.

Outcome: A repeatable checklist: gappers, news, levels, watchlist.

14

"What scanner criteria do successful day traders use? Give me 5 scan templates to test."

Why: Finding tradeable tickers is half the battle.

Outcome: Concrete scan filters for momentum, gap, and reversal setups.

15

"Explain how news catalysts move stocks — earnings, FDA, guidance, dilution, offerings. Which are tradeable, which are traps?"

Why: Catalysts make low-floats run. They also trap people on the wrong side.

Outcome: Skip the traps (offerings, dilution announcements), trade the real moves.

16

"What does 'tape reading' actually mean and how do I practice it without spending money?"

Why: Tape reading is a dying skill that still works.

Outcome: Ability to feel momentum shifts before the chart confirms.

Module 4 · Risk management (the part that keeps you alive)
17

"Teach me position sizing math. Given a $X account and Y% risk per trade, how many shares do I buy with a stop at Z?"

Why: Most traders blow up from oversizing, not bad setups.

Outcome: Reflex-level math: stop distance × shares = max loss.

18

"Explain risk/reward and win rate together — what win rate do I need at 1:1, 2:1, 3:1 to be profitable?"

Why: A 40% win rate can be profitable. A 70% win rate can lose money. The ratio matters more than the streak.

Outcome: Stop chasing win rate, start chasing expectancy.

19

"Walk me through what a 'hard stop loss' is, when to use it, and why mental stops fail."

Why: Mental stops are how good traders become bag holders.

Outcome: A rule about when stops are mandatory.

20

"What's the math of drawdown recovery? If I lose 30%, what return do I need to break even?"

Why: 30% loss needs 43% gain. 50% loss needs 100%. Drawdowns punish exponentially.

Outcome: Visceral understanding that defense > offense.

21

"Help me design daily and weekly loss limits — and what to do when I hit them."

Why: Revenge trading after losses ruins more accounts than bad strategies.

Outcome: Hard rules that take the decision out of a tilted moment.

Module 5 · Psychology & process
22

"Help me design a trading journal template — what fields should I track for each trade, and why?"

Why: You can't fix what you don't measure. Journaling is the single highest-ROI activity in trading.

Outcome: A template covering setup, emotion, execution, and lesson learned.

23

"I'll describe a losing trade — analyze it with me and tell me what I did wrong without softening it."

Why: Brutal honest review of losers is where growth happens.

Outcome: Pattern recognition for her own mistakes.

24

"Explain FOMO, revenge trading, and overtrading — what triggers them and how do I build systems against them?"

Why: The market exploits human emotion. The defense is structural, not willpower.

Outcome: Tactical rules: cooldowns, max trades per day, screen-off triggers.

25

"Build me a weekly performance review template — what metrics matter beyond P&L?"

Why: P&L lies short-term. Process metrics tell the real story.

Outcome: Win rate, avg R, plan adherence, mistake frequency.

Module 6 · Advanced topics
26

"Explain options basics in plain English — calls, puts, strikes, expiration, IV — and why most day traders should not touch them yet."

Why: Options look cheap. They're not. Understanding the trap is the first step.

Outcome: Decide intentionally if/when to add options to her toolkit.

27

"Teach me how to backtest a strategy manually — what's the process and where do most people screw it up?"

Why: Forward-testing alone is too slow. Manual backtesting builds intuition fast.

Outcome: Validated stats on her own setups before risking real money on size.

28

"What are short squeezes, gamma squeezes, and short ladder attacks — how do I spot them developing?"

Why: These are the big-payout events. They also burn people who chase late.

Outcome: Distinguish a real squeeze setup from social-media hype.

29

"Explain how taxes work for day traders — wash sales, short-term gains, and mark-to-market election."

Why: A profitable year can become a tax nightmare without planning.

Outcome: Know what to track and when to talk to a CPA.

30

"Play devil's advocate — argue why I should quit day trading. Be honest about the base rates."

Why: Stress-testing the decision keeps her sharp and honest about expectations.

Outcome: A grounded view of survivor bias and what makes the small % who succeed different.

How to use this: Pick one question per day. Don't rush. After AI answers, have it quiz you on what you just learned. Keep a journal of your answers — that's the real syllabus.
